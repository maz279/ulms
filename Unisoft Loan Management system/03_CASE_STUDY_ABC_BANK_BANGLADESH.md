## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

ABC Bank Bangladesh, a leading private commercial bank with 65 branches nationwide, faced significant challenges in their loan management operations. Manual processing, lengthy approval cycles, and increasing regulatory compliance requirements were hampering growth and customer satisfaction.

This case study examines how ABC Bank partnered with Unisoft Systems Limited to implement the Unisoft Loan Management System (ULMS), achieving remarkable results including **85% reduction in loan processing time**, **60% improvement in customer satisfaction**, and **20% reduction in NPA ratio** within the first year of implementation.

## 6. LESSONS LEARNED

### Key Success Factors

#### 1. Executive Sponsorship

**Critical Success Factor:** Active involvement from ABC Bank's Managing Director and Head of Retail Banking.

**Impact:**
- Rapid decision-making on blockers
- Resource allocation prioritization
- Organization-wide adoption encouragement

#### 2. Change Management

**Approach:**
- Comprehensive communication plan
- Regular town hall meetings
- Feedback channels for users
- Celebration of quick wins

**Result:** 98% user adoption within 3 months

#### 3. Phased Rollout

**Strategy:**
- Started with 5 pilot branches
- Collected feedback and refined
- Expanded in waves of 15-20 branches
- Learned and improved between waves

**Benefit:** Controlled risk, continuous improvement

#### 4. Strong Partnership

**Collaboration:**
- Weekly steering committee meetings
- Transparent communication
- Joint problem-solving approach
- Shared success metrics

**Outcome:** Delivered on time and within budget

### Challenges Overcome

| Challenge | Impact | Resolution |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Resistance to Change** | Initial user reluctance | Training, champions, quick wins |
| **CBS Integration Complexity** | Technical delays | Dedicated integration specialist team |
| **Data Migration Issues** | Some data inconsistencies | Data cleansing, validation scripts |
| **Branch Readiness** | Varying branch capabilities | Additional training for lagging branches |
| **Performance Optimization** | Initial slowness under load | Database tuning, caching added |

### Recommendations for Future Implementations

1. **Start Change Management Early** - Begin 3 months before technical implementation
2. **Identify & Empower Champions** - Early adopters in each department
3. **Invest in Training** - More training than you think necessary
4. **Plan for Hypercare** - Intensive support for first 4-6 weeks
5. **Celebrate Success** - Recognize and reward adoption
