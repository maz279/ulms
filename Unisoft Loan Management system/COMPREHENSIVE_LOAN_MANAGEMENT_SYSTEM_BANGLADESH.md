## REGULATORY COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

### Bangladesh Bank Comprehensive Regulatory Framework

Bangladesh's banking sector operates under a comprehensive regulatory framework:

| Regulation | Purpose | Key Requirements |
|------------|---------|------------------|
| **Bangladesh Bank Order, 1972** | Central bank authority and operations | Monetary policy, supervision |
| **Bank Company Act, 1991** | Banking operations regulation | Licensing, capital requirements |
| **Money Laundering Prevention Act, 2012** | AML/CFT requirements | Customer due diligence, reporting |
| **Anti-Terrorism Act, 2009** | Counter-terrorism financing compliance | Sanction screening |
| **Basel III RBCA Guidelines** | Capital adequacy | 12.5% CAR by 2026 |
| **BFIU e-KYC Guidelines** | Digital customer onboarding | NID verification, biometrics |
| **BRPD Circulars** | Loan classification and provisioning | 7-stage classification |
| **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2024** | Digital payments framework | Cashless transaction targets |

### BRPD Circular 15/2024 - Loan Classification Framework

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025

The new **seven-stage classification framework**:

| Stage | Classification | Overdue Days | Provisioning Rate |
|-------|---------------|--------------|-------------------|
| STD-0 | Standard (Current) | 0 days | 1% |
| STD-1 | Standard (Watch) | 1-30 days | 1% |
| STD-2 | Standard (Caution) | 31-60 days | 1% |
| SMA | Special Mention Account | 61-90 days | 5% |
| SS | Substandard | 91-180 days | 20% |
| DF | Doubtful | 181-360 days | 50% |
| B/L | Bad/Loss | >360 days | 100% |

**System Requirements:**
- Automatic DPD (Days Past Due) calculation
- Automatic classification migration
- Provisioning calculation and posting
- CL-1 to CL-5 regulatory report generation
- Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW) submission

### IFRS-9 ECL Compliance (Mandatory by December 2027)

**Expected Credit Loss Model:**

| Stage | Condition | ECL Calculation |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Stage 1** | Good standing | 12-month ECL (1% general provision) |
| **Stage 2** | Significant credit risk increase | Lifetime ECL (higher provisions) |
| **Stage 3** | Credit impaired | Lifetime ECL (100% specific provision) |

**Implementation Requirements:**
- Forward-looking macroeconomic scenario modeling
- Probability of Default (PD) calculation
- Loss Given Default (LGD) estimation
- Exposure at Default (EAD) measurement
- Historical data analysis (minimum 5 years)

### Credit Information Bureau (CIB) Integration

**Mandatory Requirements:**

- Online integration since July 2011
- Reporting threshold: BDT 1 (one) and above
- Monthly batch contributions required
- Real-time updates for loan modifications
- Collateral Information System integration
- Two-year historical data distribution

**CIB Report Components:**
- Borrower classification status (STD, SMA, SS, DF, B/L)
- Total outstanding balance
- Group/related party exposure
- Guarantor information
- Historical payment behavior (24 months)

### Basel III Capital Adequacy Requirements

**Key Requirements:**

- **Risk-Weighted Asset (RWA)** calculation
- **Credit Risk** assessment (Standardized/IRB approach)
- **Operational Risk** measurement (Basic/Standardized/Advanced)
- **Market Risk** evaluation
- **Capital Conservation Buffer** tracking
- **12.5% Capital Adequacy Ratio** target by 2026

### AML/KYC Compliance

**BFIU Guidelines:**

- Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk customers
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) screening
- Sanction list checking
- Transaction monitoring
- Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) filing

**e-KYC Integration:**
- NID database verification
- Biometric authentication
- Address verification
- Photo matching
- Electronic signature capture

### Green Banking Requirements

- **5% minimum** green financing target
- Green loan product tracking
- Environmental impact assessment
- Sustainability reporting
