## 4. Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) & Support Fees (Years 2–5)

* **Year 1 Maintenance:** Completely **FREE of charge** under our comprehensive 12-month warranty period.
* **Years 2 to 5 Maintenance:** Fixed at exactly **20% of the Net Software License Value** (Schedule A) annually:

$$\text{Annual AMC Fee} = 20\% \times \text{BDT } 2,50,00,000 = \mathbf{BDT\ 50,00,000\ /\ Year\ (+15\%\ VAT)}$$

### Scope of Annual Maintenance Contract Coverage:
1. **Free Bangladesh Bank Regulatory Updates:** All mandatory updates driven by new BRPD, BFIU, or CIB circulars are delivered at zero additional customization cost.
2. **Dedicated Dhaka Support Engineering:** Tier-1 helpdesk, Tier-2 engineering, and Tier-3 architect escalations with 30-minute on-site response in Dhaka.
3. **Core Version Upgrades:** All minor and major software version updates and security patches released by Unisoft during the active maintenance term.
