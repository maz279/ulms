## 1. Commercial Architecture & Value Principles

Unisoft’s pricing architecture for **ULMS v2.0** eliminates the hidden consulting traps, offshore per-diem fees, and foreign exchange volatility typical of global legacy packages.

### Four Binding Commercial Pillars:
1. **100% Domestic Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) Invoicing:** Zero US Dollar exposure, zero central bank outward remittance delays, and 100% eligible NBR VAT input tax credits via UniVAT™.
2. **Transparent Perpetual Enterprise Licensing:** Bank owns an irrevocable perpetual license to operate the platform across all sovereign datacenters without per-seat employee surcharges.
3. **Turnkey Fixed-Price Implementation:** Pre-defined milestone delivery guaranteeing no scope-creep change orders for agreed requirements.
4. **Capped Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC):** Year 1 warranty is included free (0%); Years 2 through 5 are fixed at exactly **20% of the initial software license fee**.
