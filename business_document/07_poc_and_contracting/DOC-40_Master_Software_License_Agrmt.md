## 2. Grant of License & Operational Scope

1. **Scope of Grant:** Subject to the payment of the agreed Perpetual Software License Fee (Schedule A of `DOC-24`), Licensor hereby grants to Licensee a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable enterprise license to install, operate, and utilize ULMS v2.0.
2. **Authorized Environment:** Licensee is authorized to run the software on its primary on-premise datacenter, secondary disaster recovery (DR) site, and internal development/UAT environments located within the sovereign territory of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.
3. **Usage Volume:** The license permits unlimited staff user seats and unlimited branch processing across Licensee's domestic branch and agent banking network.

---

## 3. Intellectual Property Rights & Software Ownership

1. **Retained Ownership:** Licensor retains all worldwide right, title, and interest, including all patents, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks, and other intellectual property rights in and to ULMS v2.0, its source code, and associated architectural documentation.
2. **Customer Data Sovereignty:** Licensee retains exclusive, unencumbered ownership of all customer records, transaction histories, financial ledgers, and loan data processed by the software. Under no circumstances shall Licensor claim ownership over Bank data.

---

## 4. Regulatory Warranties & Bangladesh Bank Compliance

1. **Statutory Regulatory Warranty:** Licensor explicitly warrants that ULMS v2.0 natively satisfies all applicable mandatory requirements of:
   * Bangladesh Bank **BRPD Circular No. 15/2024** (7-stage credit classification, DPD tracking, statutory provisioning);
   * Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit (**BFIU**) Circulars 25, 26, and 31 (e-KYC biometric verification and AML/CFT guidelines);
   * Bangladesh Bank Credit Information Bureau (**CIB**) Online REST integration specifications.
2. **Free Regulatory Patch Guarantee:** During any active Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) period, Licensor covenants to deliver mandatory software updates to maintain compliance with future Bangladesh Bank BRPD circulars at **zero additional license fee**.

---

## 5. Warranties, Support & Defect Rectification

1. **12-Month Comprehensive Warranty:** Licensor warrants that for a period of twelve (12) months following formal User Acceptance Testing (UAT) sign-off, ULMS v2.0 shall operate substantially in conformance with the functional specifications set forth in `DOC-26_Functional_Compliance_Matrix.md`.
2. **Rectification SLA:** Any Priority-1 defect (system down or core CBS integration failure) reported by Licensee shall be responded to within fifteen (15) minutes and restored within two (2) hours by Licensor’s engineering team in Dhaka.

---

## 6. Limitation of Liability & Indemnification

1. **Liability Cap:** Except for breaches of confidentiality or gross negligence, neither party's aggregate cumulative liability arising under this Agreement shall exceed the total license fee paid by Licensee under this Agreement.
2. **IP Infringement Indemnity:** Licensor agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Licensee against any third-party claim alleging that the software infringes any valid copyright, patent, or intellectual property right enforceable in Bangladesh.

---

## 7. Source Code Escrow Agreement

To provide absolute institutional continuity for Licensee:
1. Licensor agrees to deposit a copy of the ULMS v2.0 source code, build scripts, and database schema definitions with an accredited commercial **Source Code Escrow Agent** in Dhaka.
2. The escrow deposit shall be released to Licensee solely upon the occurrence of a verified Release Condition (such as Licensor entering formal corporate bankruptcy or permanently ceasing operations without assigning maintenance obligations).

---

## 8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

1. **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by, construed, and enforced in accordance with the substantive laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.
2. **Arbitration:** Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of this Agreement that cannot be resolved amicably within thirty (30) days shall be referred to binding arbitration conducted in Dhaka under the rules of the **Bangladesh International Arbitration Centre (BIAC)** or the **Dhaka Chamber of Commerce and Industry (DCCI)**.
