### 2.2 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Formula | Target | Current |
|-----|---------|--------|---------|
| Portfolio Growth | (Current - Previous) / Previous | > 10% YoY | 15% |
| NPL Ratio | NPL / Total Portfolio | < 5% | 3.2% |
| Provision Coverage | Total Provision / NPL | > 100% | 120% |
| Cost of Risk | Provision / Average Portfolio | < 2% | 1.5% |
| ROA (Loans) | Net Income / Average Assets | > 2% | 2.3% |

## 5. Risk Management

- Portfolio growth decline > 20%
- NPL increase > 0.5% monthly
- Single borrower limit breach

## 7. Decision Support

### 7.1 What-If Analysis

1. Navigate to **Management** → **Simulation**
2. Adjust parameters:
   - Interest rate changes
   - Growth scenarios
   - NPL scenarios
3. View impact on:
   - Revenue
   - Provisions
   - Capital adequacy

### 7.2 Approval Workflow

1. **Management** → **Approvals**
2. Review pending items:
   - Large exposures > limit
   - Policy exceptions
   - Write-off recommendations
3. Approve/Reject with comments
